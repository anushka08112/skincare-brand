About GlowCare

GlowCare is a skincare e-commerce platform designed to help users find the right products based on skin concerns and ingredients.

Features

  • Shop by Skin Concern (Acne, Dry Skin, Pigmentation)
  • Shop by Ingredients (Vitamin C, Niacinamide, etc.)
  • Product Reviews System
  • Cart & Checkout System
  • Personalized Product Discovery

Technology Used

  • Flask (Backend)
  • MySQL (Database)
  • HTML, CSS (Frontend)
  • JavaScript (Cart functionality)
